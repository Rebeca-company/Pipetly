# **Primary Cell Culture and Alginate Encapsulation Protocol for Pituitary Adenoma**

## **Tumor Sample Processing**

1. Obtain the pituitary adenoma tissue biopsy through a trans-sphenoidal surgical procedure1.  
2. Place the collected sample immediately into a sterile glass bottle containing 50 ml of fresh M199 culture medium enriched with 10% fetal bovine serum, 0.6 mg/ml sodium bicarbonate, 2.4 mg/ml HEPES, and 1% penicillin/streptomycin with a pH of 7.41.  
3. Maintain the glass bottle with the sample on ice during transport to the laboratory, considering that the tumor tissue can be processed up to several hours after surgery1.  
4. Filter all dissociation and alginate solutions through a 0.22 µm membrane before use1.  
5. Place the tumor tissue inside a laminar flow cabinet in a 35 mm Petri dish filled with approximately 20 ml of calcium- and magnesium-free phosphate-buffered saline at pH 7.41.  
6. Hold the tissue with surgical tweezers, transfer it to a second Petri dish containing fresh calcium- and magnesium-free phosphate-buffered saline, and wash it gently to remove debris and red blood cells1.  
7. Hold the cleaned tissue with tweezers and cut it into small pieces using small surgical scissors1.  
8. Transfer the tissue fragments along with the phosphate-buffered saline into a 15 ml tube using a Pasteur pipette with rounded, flame-polished edges1.  
9. Centrifuge the tube at 68 × g for 10 minutes at room temperature1.  
10. Carefully aspirate the supernatant using a Pasteur pipette and discard it1.

## **Enzymatic Dissociation**

11. Add 3 to 5 ml of 1% type I collagenase solution to the tube, using approximately 5 ml for every 65 mm³ of tissue1.  
12. Mix the content by inverting the tube two to three times1.  
13. Incubate the tissue in the collagenase solution for 30 minutes at 37 °C under constant rotation1.  
14. Centrifuge the tube at 68 × g for 10 minutes at room temperature1.  
15. Aspirate the collagenase supernatant with a Pasteur pipette and discard it1.  
16. Add 5 ml of DNAse solution, composed of 20 µl of DNAse I at 134 U/ml dissolved in 4 ml of 0.1% soy trypsin inhibitor, and mix by inverting the tube two to three times1.  
17. Centrifuge the tube at 68 × g for 10 minutes at room temperature1.  
18. Perform a second enzymatic digestion using a 0.01% trypsin solution for 3 minutes at 37 °C if the tissue fragments have not completely dissociated1.  
19. Stop this secondary digestion immediately by adding 2 volumes of trypsin inhibitor solution1.  
20. Aspirate the supernatant with a Pasteur pipette and discard it1.  
21. Resuspend the remaining cell pellet in 1 volume of 1.2 M EGTA solution1.

## **Bead Formation and Primary Cell Culture**

22. Add 2 volumes of 1.2% sodium alginate solution to the cell suspension1.  
23. Gently mix the cell suspension and alginate by pipetting up and down with a Pasteur pipette1.  
24. Remove the plunger from a sterile syringe equipped with a 21 G needle1.  
25. Load the alginate and cell mixture into the syringe barrel using a pipette1.  
26. Gently reinsert the plunger into the syringe1.  
27. Dispense the alginate and cell solution drop by drop into a glass beaker containing approximately 30 ml of 102 mM calcium chloride solution1.  
28. Maintain the syringe needle positioned about 5 cm above the surface of the calcium chloride solution during dispensing1.  
29. Allow the falling alginate and cell suspension to gel instantly upon contact with the calcium chloride solution to form spherical beads1.  
30. Keep the newly formed beads submerged in the calcium chloride solution for exactly 5 minutes1.  
31. Stir the beaker gently in circular motions by hand during this period to prevent the alginate beads from adhering to each other1.  
32. Carefully remove the calcium chloride solution using a Pasteur pipette1.  
33. Wash the alginate beads twice, using 3 to 5 ml of M199 culture medium enriched with 20% fetal bovine serum for each wash1.  
34. Transfer the washed alginate beads to a standard T25 tissue culture flask using a sterile spatula1.  
35. Add 4 to 5 ml of M199 culture medium enriched with 20% fetal bovine serum to the T25 flask1.  
36. Place the culture flask in a humidified incubator at 37 °C with 5% carbon dioxide to establish the three-dimensional culture, and renew the culture medium every three days to maintain long-term cell viability1.
