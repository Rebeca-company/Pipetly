# **Generation of Transgene-Free Tomato Lines using CRISPR-Cas9**

## **Explant Preparation**

1. Seed germination  
   * Sow the sterilized seeds on a solid basal medium1.  
   * Incubate the plates in the dark at 25 °C for 3 to 4 days3.  
   * Transfer the cultures to a 16-hour light and 8-hour dark photoperiod at 25 °C for an additional 4 to 6 days1.  
2. Cotyledon excision  
   * Excise the cotyledons from 7 to 10-day-old seedlings, cutting at the petiole1.  
   * Trim and discard the distal tips of the cotyledons1.  
   * Transversely divide the remaining tissue into sections of approximately 1 cm2.  
3. Explant preculture  
   * Place the cotyledon sections with the adaxial side down on a solid preculture medium8.  
   * Incubate the explants at 25 °C under a 16-hour light photoperiod for 1 to 2 days2.

## **Inoculation and Co-cultivation**

4. Adjustment of the *Agrobacterium* suspension  
   * Centrifuge the liquid bacterial culture at 8000 rpm for 10 minutes at 20 °C2.  
   * Resuspend the pellet in sterile liquid medium until reaching an optical density (OD600) of 0.5 to 0.62.  
5. Plant tissue infection  
   * Immerse the precultured explants in the *Agrobacterium* suspension for 5 to 20 minutes applying occasional agitation11.  
   * Remove the explants from the bacterial suspension2.  
   * Briefly dry the excess liquid by placing the explants on sterile filter paper2.  
6. Co-cultivation phase  
   * Transfer the inoculated explants, maintaining the adaxial side down, to plates with solid co-cultivation medium5.  
   * Seal the plates and incubate in the dark at a temperature of 19 °C to 25 °C for 48 to 72 hours2.

## **Regeneration, Selection, and Rooting**

7. Transfer to selection medium  
   * Transfer the co-cultivated explants, inverting their position so the adaxial side faces up, to a primary selective regeneration medium2.  
   * Seal the plates with porous tape and incubate at 25 °C under a 16-hour light and 8-hour dark photoperiod11.  
8. Subculture and shoot elongation  
   * Transfer the entire explants to fresh selective regeneration medium every 14 to 21 days1.  
   * Excise the individual adventitious shoots from the primary callus once they reach a minimum length of 2 cm and include at least one node11.  
9. Root induction  
   * Transfer the excised shoots to sterile vessels containing rooting medium8.  
   * Maintain the shoots under a 16-hour light photoperiod11.  
   * Discard the shoots that do not develop root networks after 21 days10.

## **Acclimatization and Genotypic Verification (T0 Generation)**

10. Plantlet acclimatization  
    * Remove the rooted plantlets from the *in vitro* vessels and wash the roots with sterile water1.  
    * Transplant the plantlets into pots with moistened soil and immediately cover with humidity domes or clear polyethylene bags4.  
    * Progressively open the humidity domes over the course of 5 to 7 days14.  
11. DNA extraction and transformation validation  
    * Collect leaf tissue from the acclimatized T0 plants12.  
    * Extract genomic DNA following a standard protocol or using commercial kits18.  
    * Run a polymerase chain reaction (PCR) using specific primers to amplify the Cas9 sequence or the resistance marker20.  
12. Mutation and gene editing analysis  
    * Amplify via PCR the genomic sequences flanking the guide RNA target sites20.  
    * Sequence the obtained amplicons and align the sequences with the wild-type reference to identify insertions, deletions, or biallelic mutations12.

## **Segregation and Isolation of Transgene-Free Plants (T1 Generation)**

13. T1 generation propagation  
    * Grow the T0 plants confirmed as edited to the flowering stage and allow them to self-pollinate18.  
    * Collect, process, and dry the mature T1 generation seeds12.  
14. Visual transgene detection (Applicable if fluorescent markers were used)  
    * Observe the dry T1 seeds under a stereomicroscope equipped with the fluorescence filter corresponding to the marker integrated into the vector24.  
    * Separate and retain exclusively the seeds that do not emit fluorescence22.  
15. Germination and isolation of null segregants  
    * Germinate a minimum of 10 selected T1 seeds for each independent transformation event12.  
    * Extract genomic DNA from the leaf tissue of the germinated T1 plantlets12.  
    * Perform a PCR with specific primers for the Cas9 gene; identify and keep only the individuals with negative amplification results15.  
16. Confirmation of homozygosity in cassette-free lines  
    * Amplify the original target locus in the transgene-free T1 individuals12.  
    * Sequence the PCR products to select the plants that present the desired genetic modification in a homozygous state12.
