### Protocol Steps

1. Wash the tumor tissue:
   1.1. Inside a laminar flow cabinet, place the previously-obtained adenoma tumor tissue in a 35 mm dish containing approximately 20 mL of calcium- and magnesium-free PBS.
   1.2. Using surgical tweezers, transfer the tissue into another petri dish containing fresh PBS to wash.
   1.3. Repeat the transfer into fresh buffer until red blood cells and debris are removed.

2. Mince the tissue:
   2.1. With surgical tweezers, hold the pituitary adenoma tissue.
   2.2. Using a small surgical scissor, cut the tissue into small pieces.
   2.3. Using a Pasteur pipette with rounded edges, transfer the tissue fragments and buffer into a 15 mL tube.

3. Perform the first centrifugation:
   3.1. Centrifuge at 68 × g at room temperature for 10 minutes.
   3.2. Use a Pasteur pipette to remove the PBS.

4. Digest the tissue enzymatically:
   4.1. Add 3–5 mL of collagenase solution.
   4.2. Mix 2–3 times by inverting the tube.
   4.3. Incubate the tissue in the collagenase at 37 °C under constant rotation for 30 minutes.

5. Remove collagenase and treat with TNase:
   5.1. Centrifuge the tube again at 68 × g for 10 minutes.
   5.2. Use a Pasteur pipette to remove the collagenase solution.
   5.3. Add 5 mL of TNase.
   5.4. Mix 2–3 times by inverting the tube.
   5.5. Centrifuge again at 68 × g for 10 minutes.

6. Check dissociation completeness:
   6.1. If the tissue is not completely dissociated, perform a second enzymatic digestion (repeat steps 4–5 according to the text protocol).

7. Prepare cell-alginate mixture:
   7.1. Aspirate the supernatant and discard it.
   7.2. Resuspend the pellet with one volume of EGTA solution.
   7.3. Gently mix with two volumes of alginate solution.

8. Form alginate beads:
   8.1. Remove the plunger from a sterile syringe containing a 21‑gauge needle.
   8.2. Use a pipette to load the alginate and cell suspension into the syringe.
   8.3. Gently insert the plunger into the syringe.
   8.4. Place the syringe needle approximately 5 cm from the surface of the calcium chloride solution in a glass beaker.
   8.5. Carefully dispense the alginate cell suspension drop‑by‑drop into the beaker. The suspension will jell on contact to form spherical beads.
   8.6. Using circular motions, slowly stir the glass beaker to prevent the beads from sticking.
   8.7. Keep the alginate beads in the calcium chloride solution for 5 minutes.

9. Wash and transfer beads:
   9.1. Carefully remove the calcium solution.
   9.2. Wash the beads twice with 3–5 mL of M199 enriched with 20% FBS.
   9.3. With a sterile spatula, transfer the alginate beads into a regular T25 tissue culture flask.
   9.4. Add 4–5 mL of M199 with FBS.

10. Incubate for long-term culture:
    10.1. Incubate at 37 °C with 5% CO₂ in a humidified incubator.
