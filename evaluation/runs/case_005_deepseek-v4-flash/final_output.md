**Source:** The enhanced osteogenic differentiation of human periodontal ligament stem cells and M2 polarization of macrophages may be mediated by EphB4/ephrinB2 signaling pathway: a study of their direct co-culture.

**DOI:** [10.1186/s13287-026-04946-1](https://doi.org/10.1186/s13287-026-04946-1)

**Relevance score:** 100.0/100

### Protocol Steps

1. Plate THP-1 cells in a 6-well plate at a density of 1 × 10⁶ cells per well in RPMI-1640 medium supplemented with 10% FBS.
   a. Use THP-1 cells from a single source (e.g., Procell, China).
   b. Use 6-well plates (e.g., Servicebio, China).
2. Induce macrophage differentiation by adding phorbol-12-myristate-13-acetate (PMA) to a final concentration of 100 ng/mL.
   a. Use PMA from a single lot (e.g., Sigma; Merck, Germany; Cat# P1585).
   b. Incubate for 48 hours.
3. After 48 hours, remove the original medium and wash the cells three times with PBS to remove residual PMA.
4. Add fresh complete medium (RPMI-1640 supplemented with 10% FBS) and maintain the cells in stationary culture for an additional 24 hours.
5. For all experiments, include at least three biologically independent replicates, and perform each experiment in technical triplicate.

## Rank 2 — Protocol
**Source:** Induced pluripotent stem cell-derived mesenchymal stem cells (iMSCs) inhibit M1 macrophage polarization and reduce alveolar bone loss associated with periodontitis

**DOI:** [10.1186/s13287-025-04327-0](https://doi.org/10.1186/s13287-025-04327-0)

**Relevance score:** 98.0/100

### Protocol Steps

1. Maintain THP-1 cell culture in RPMI 1640 medium supplemented with 10% fetal bovine serum (FBS), 1% penicillin/streptomycin, and 0.05 nM 2‑mercaptoethanol at 37 °C in a 5% CO₂ incubator.
   a. Replace the culture medium every other day.
   b. Passage the cells every four days.

2. Differentiate THP-1 cells into M0 macrophages.
   a. Seed the THP-1 cells in culture medium.
   b. Add 100 ng/mL Phorbol 12‑Myristate 13‑Acetate (PMA) to the medium.
   c. Incubate for 48 hours at 37 °C in a 5% CO₂ incubator.
   d. After 48 hours, remove the PMA‑containing medium by exchanging with fresh culture medium.

3. Polarize the M0 macrophages into the M1 phenotype.
   a. Add 20 ng/mL IFN‑γ and 100 ng/mL LPS from *P. gingivalis* to the culture medium.
   b. Incubate for an additional 48 hours at 37 °C in a 5% CO₂ incubator.

## Rank 3 — Protocol
**Source:** Macrophage Differentiation and Polarization into an M2-Like Phenotype using a Human Monocyte-Like THP-1 Leukemia Cell Line.

**DOI:** [10.3791/62652](https://doi.org/10.3791/62652)

**Relevance score:** 95.0/100

### Protocol Steps

1.  **Differentiate THP‑1 cells into “young M0” macrophages.**
    1.  Culture THP‑1 cells in RPMI‑1640 medium supplemented with 10% fetal bovine serum (FBS), 10,000 units/mL penicillin, 10 mg/mL streptomycin, and 25 μg/mL amphotericin B at 37°C with 5% CO₂.
    2.  Seed cells at a density of 3 × 10⁵ cells/mL into a 24‑well culture plate.
    3.  Add 100 ng/mL phorbol 12‑myristate 13‑acetate (PMA) and incubate for 72 h to induce macrophage differentiation.

2.  **Allow differentiated cells to rest.**
    1.  Remove the medium containing PMA.
    2.  Add fresh culture medium without PMA and incubate for 48 h.

3.  **Generate M0 macrophage controls (“aged M0”).**
    1.  After the 48‑h rest period, aspirate the medium and replace with fresh culture medium.
    2.  Incubate for an additional 5 days without adding any interleukin. Do not proceed to Step 4.

4.  **Polarize differentiated macrophages into an M2‑like anti‑inflammatory phenotype.**
    1.  After the 48‑h rest period, aspirate the medium and add fresh culture medium.
    2.  Add 20 ng/mL interleukin‑4 (IL‑4) and 20 ng/mL interleukin‑13 (IL‑13).
    3.  Incubate for 96 h to obtain polarized M2‑like macrophages. [{10.3791/62652}](https://doi.org/10.3791/62652)
