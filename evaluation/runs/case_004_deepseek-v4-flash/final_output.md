## Rank 1 — Protocol
**Source:** Multiplex Genome Editing of Human Pluripotent Stem Cells Using Cpf1.

**DOI:** [10.21769/bioprotoc.5108](https://doi.org/10.21769/bioprotoc.5108)

**Relevance score:** 60.0/100

### Protocol Steps

1. Obtain the AAVS1-tdT targeting plasmid from Addgene (#194728) and purify it.
2. Transfect HEK293T cells (one well of a 12-well plate) with 0.8 μg of the purified AAVS1-CAGGS-tdTomato targeting vector and 0.4 μg of AsCpf1-AAVS1 plasmid using Lipofectamine 2000 transfection reagent.
3. Three days post-transfection, sort GFP and tdTomato (tdT) double-positive cells on a FACSAria.
4. Plate the sorted cells at a density of approximately 2,000 cells per 10 cm culture plate and subject them to selection with 2 μg/mL puromycin until colonies form.
5. Mechanically passage individual tdT-expressing colonies to individual wells of a 12-well plate for expansion.
6. Genotype the expanded HEK293T clones for correct knockin:
   - Extract 0.5 μg of genomic DNA from each clone.
   - Perform a PCR reaction using primers 495 (5′-TCTCTCTCCTGAGTCCGGACC-3′) and 489 (5′-ACTGAGCTCTCAGGCACCGGGCTTGCGG-3′) with REDTaq PCR Reaction Mix under the following conditions: 95 °C for 5 min; 35 cycles of 95 °C for 30 s, 57 °C for 30 s, 72 °C for 1 min 40 s; final extension at 72 °C for 5 min.
   - Resolve the PCR products on a 1% agarose gel.
   - Excise bands of the expected size and extract DNA using a gel purification kit (Omega Bio-tek).
   - Subject the purified DNA to Sanger sequencing using primer 495.

## Rank 2 — Protocol
**Source:** Multiplex genome editing of human pluripotent stem cells using Cpf1

**DOI:** [10.1101/2022.04.13.488123](https://doi.org/10.1101/2022.04.13.488123)

**Relevance score:** 35.0/100

### Protocol Steps

1. **Prepare cells for electroporation**
   1. One day before electroporation, culture human pluripotent stem cells (hPSCs) in medium supplemented with 10 μM Rho kinase (ROCK) inhibitor Y‑27632 (Thermo Fisher Scientific) [{10.1016/j.xcrm.2020.100097}](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.xcrm.2020.100097).
   2. On the day of electroporation, dissociate approximately 10 million hPSCs into single cells using Accutase (StemCell Technologies).
   3. Filter the cell suspension through a 40 µm cell strainer (Corning).
   4. Wash the cells with medium containing Y‑27632, then centrifuge and discard the supernatant.
   5. Resuspend the cell pellet in 800 µL of PBS containing:
      - 100 µg of sg‑AAVS1 Cpf1 plasmid (or 20 µg of sg‑AAVS1 SpCas9 plasmid), and
      - 40 µg of donor construct (AAVS1‑targeting donor).

2. **Electroporate cells**
   1. Transfer the cell‑DNA mixture to a 0.4‑cm cuvette (BioRad).
   2. Incubate the cuvette on ice for 5 minutes.
   3. Electroporate using a Gene Pulser Xcell System (Bio‑Rad) with a single pulse of 250 V, 500 µF.
   4. Immediately after electroporation, incubate the cuvette on ice for another 5 minutes.

3. **Plate electroporated cells**
   1. Plate the entire electroporated cell suspension onto two 6‑well plates pre‑seeded with DR4 mouse embryonic fibroblasts (MEF) [{10.1016/j.xcrm.2020.100097}](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.xcrm.2020.100097).

4. **Select for successfully targeted cells**
   1. 3–4 days after electroporation, add puromycin to the culture medium at a final concentration of 0.5 µg/mL.

5. **Isolate and expand individual clones**
   1. Manually pick individual puromycin‑resistant colonies and transfer each to a well of a MEF‑coated 12‑well plate.
   2. Expand the clones until sufficient cell numbers are obtained for genotyping.

6. **Confirm correct targeting by PCR and Southern blot**
   1. **PCR genotyping**:
      - Extract genomic DNA from each expanded clone.
      - Perform PCR using primers specific for the AAVS1 locus (e.g., 5′‑GGCCTTTGGTGCAGTGACCAGAGTGTCAGG‑3′ and 5′‑GATTCTCCTCGACGTCACCGCATGTTAGC‑3′) with the following cycling conditions: 95 °C for 3 min; 40 cycles of 98 °C for 20 s, 58 °C for 30 s, 72 °C for 5.5 min; final extension at 72 °C for 10 min.
      - Purify the ~5 kb amplicon using a gel purification kit (Omega Biotech) and sequence with the forward primer to verify correct integration.
   2. **Southern blot analysis**:
      - Digest 15 µg of genomic DNA from each clone with SphI.
      - Separate the digested DNA by agarose gel electrophoresis and transfer to a Hybond‑XL membrane (GE Healthcare).
      - Hybridize the membrane with random‑primed ³²P‑dCTP‑labeled probes:
        - **5′ internal probe**: generated by PCR from the AAVS1‑tdTomato plasmid using primers 5′‑GAATTCGCCCTTTGCTTTCTCTGAC‑3′ and 5′‑TGAGCTCTCGGACCCCTGGAAGAT‑3′.
        - **3′ external probe**: generated by PCR from H1 genomic DNA using primers 5′‑ACAGGTACCATGTGGGGTTC‑3′ and 5′‑CCCTTGCCTCACCTGGCGAT‑3′.
      - Detect signals by autoradiography to confirm correct targeting at the AAVS1 locus [{10.1016/j.xcrm.2020.100097}](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.xcrm.2020.100097).

## Rank 3 — Protocol
**Source:** Enhancement of CRISPR-Cas12a system through universal circular RNA design

**DOI:** [10.1016/j.crmeth.2025.101076](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.crmeth.2025.101076)

**Relevance score:** 20.0/100

### Protocol Steps

1.  **Seed and culture HEK293T cells for transfection**
    1.  Seed HEK293T cells at a density of 1×10⁵ cells per well in a 24-well plate.
    2.  Culture cells in Dulbecco's Modified Eagle Medium (DMEM) supplemented with 10% Fetal Bovine Serum and 1% penicillin/streptomycin.
    3.  Incubate at 37°C in a 5% CO₂ atmosphere for 20 hours, until cells reach approximately 80% confluence.
2.  **Prepare the Cas12a ribonucleoprotein (RNP) complex**
    1.  Mix 250 pmol of AAVS1 crRNA (or AAVS1-Cir-crRNA) with 50 μL of Opti-MEM I. Mix gently.
    2.  Add 100 pmol of EnGen Lba LbCas12a (Cpf1) to the mixture. Mix gently.
    3.  Incubate the mixture at room temperature for 15 minutes.
3.  **Perform transfection**
    1.  Add 2 μL of the TransIT-X2 Dynamic Delivery System to the RNP complex mixture. Mix gently.
    2.  Incubate at room temperature for an additional 15 minutes.
    3.  Add the entire transfection mixture to the cells in the 24-well plate.
    4.  Gently shake the plate to ensure even distribution.
    5.  Incubate the cells at 37°C in a 5% CO₂ atmosphere.
    6.  After 6 hours of incubation, expose the cells to ultraviolet light (λ = 365 nm, 35 W) for 3 minutes.
    7.  Continue incubation for an additional 42 hours at 37°C in a 5% CO₂ atmosphere (total 48 hours post-transfection).
4.  **Extract genomic DNA**
    1.  48 hours post-transfection, extract genomic DNA from the cells using the MolPure Cell/Tissue DNA Kit.
5.  **Amplify the target genomic region by PCR**
    1.  Prepare a PCR mixture containing:
        - 5 pmol of AAVS1-PCR-F primer
        - 5 pmol of AAVS1-PCR-R primer
        - 100 ng of extracted genomic DNA
        - 1× Canace Plus PCR buffer (with Mg²⁺ and dNTPs)
        - 1 unit of Hieff Canace Plus High-Fidelity DNA Polymerase
        - Nuclease-free water to a final volume of 50 μL.
    2.  Run the PCR under the following conditions:
        - Initial denaturation: 98°C for 8 minutes
        - 40 cycles of:
            - Denaturation: 98°C for 10 seconds
            - Annealing: 60°C for 15 seconds
            - Extension: 72°C for 30 seconds
        - Final extension: 72°C for 5 minutes.
    3.  Purify the PCR product using the MolPure PCR Purification Kit.
6.  **Perform T7 Endonuclease I (T7EI) digestion**
    1.  Mix 200 ng of purified PCR product with 1× T7EI Reaction Buffer and nuclease-free water to a final volume of 19 μL.
    2.  Anneal the mixture using the following protocol:
        - 95°C for 5 minutes
        - Cool to 85°C at ~2°C/second
        - Cool to 25°C at ~0.1°C/second.
    3.  Add 1 μL of T7EI to the annealed mixture.
    4.  Incubate the reaction at 37°C for 20 minutes.
7.  **Analyze results by gel electrophoresis**
    1.  Immediately run the digestion products on a 2% agarose gel.
    2.  Quantify the gene editing efficiency by analyzing the gel image using ImageJ software. Calculate the ratio of the intensity of the cleaved bands to the total band intensity.
