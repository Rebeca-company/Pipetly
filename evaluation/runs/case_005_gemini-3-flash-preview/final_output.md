### Protocol Steps

1. **THP-1 Suspension Culture and Maintenance**
    1. Seed THP-1 cells at a density of 200,000 cells/mL in basal RPMI media supplemented with 10% FBS, 1 mM Sodium Pyruvate, and 1% antibiotic-antimycotic.
    2. Maintain cells in a humidified incubator at 37 °C and 5% CO2.
    3. Replace the culture medium every 2–3 days.
    4. Passage the cells before they reach a maximum density of 1,000,000 cells/mL.

2. **Macrophage Differentiation**
    1. Seed 400,000 THP-1 cells per well in a 12-well plate using RPMI media (10% FBS, 1% antibiotic-antimycotic, 1 mM Sodium Pyruvate).
    2. Add 100 ng/mL PMA to the culture media to induce differentiation.
    3. Incubate the cells for 48 hours.

3. **LPS Stimulation and EV Treatment**
    1. Remove the PMA-containing media.
    2. Stimulate the THP-1 derived macrophages with 500 ng/mL LPS in serum-free RPMI media (supplemented with 1% antibiotic-antimycotic).
    3. Incubate for 2 hours.
    4. Add the isolated EV suspension (NTC-EVs or TSG-6-EVs) directly to the wells.
        1. For standard assays, apply a dosage of 1,000 particles/cell.
        2. For dose-dependency assays, apply dosages of 500, 1,000, or 2,000 particles/cell.
    5. Co-incubate the cells with the EV suspension for 24 hours.

4. **Sample Collection**
    1. Collect the cell culture media supernatants 24 hours post-treatment.
    2. Harvest the cell RNA for downstream analysis.

### Protocol for THP-1 Expansion, Differentiation, and Conditioned Medium Preparation

1.  **Cell Culture and Maintenance**
    1.  Prepare the complete growth medium using RPMI 1640 supplemented with 10% non-heat-treated FBS, 2 mM L-glutamine, 0.05 mM β-mercaptoethanol, 10 mM HEPES, 4500 mg/L glucose, 100 U/ml penicillin, and 100 μg/ml streptomycin.
    2.  Maintain THP-1 cells at 37°C in a humidified 5% CO₂ atmosphere.
    3.  Upon thawing, expand cells by adding fresh medium every 48 hours until the culture reaches a density of 8 × 10⁵ cells/ml.
    4.  Perform regular passages every 2 days with a complete medium replacement once the maximum density of 8 × 10⁵ cells/ml is reached.
    5.  Ensure the cell density never drops below a minimum of 3 × 10⁵ cells/ml.

2.  **Monocyte Differentiation**
    1.  Induce differentiation by exposing THP-1 cells to 185 ng/ml phorbol 12-myristate 13-acetate (PMA) in DMSO for 6 hours.

3.  **Macrophage Polarization**
    1.  For M1 polarization: Incubate differentiated cells for 48 hours in growth medium containing 185 ng/ml PMA, 20 ng/ml INF-γ, and 100 ng/ml LPS [[10.1111/j.1365-2133.2009.09271.x](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1365-2133.2009.09271.x); [10.1016/j.vascn.2014.01.003](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.vascn.2014.01.003)].
    2.  For M2 polarization: Incubate differentiated cells for 48 hours in growth medium containing 185 ng/ml PMA, 20 ng/ml IL-4, and 20 ng/ml IL-13 [[10.1111/j.1365-2133.2009.09271.x](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1365-2133.2009.09271.x); [10.1016/j.vascn.2014.01.003](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.vascn.2014.01.003)].
    3.  For Resting (M0) condition: Incubate differentiated cells for 48 hours in growth medium containing 185 ng/ml PMA only.

4.  **Conditioned Medium (CM) Collection and Processing**
    1.  Wash cells to remove stimuli and PMA.
    2.  Incubate polarized (M1, M2) and resting (M0) cells in serum-free RPMI without any additional stimuli or PMA for 72 hours.
    3.  Collect the media and concentrate it 10-fold using Amicon Ultra-15 centrifugal filter units (3 KDa cut-off).
    4.  Determine total protein content of the concentrated CM via Bradford assay using bovine serum albumin as a reference.
    5.  Store the processed CM at -20°C until required for assays.

### Protocol Steps

1. **THP-1 Cell Culture and Maintenance**
    1. Prepare the complete growth medium by supplementing RPMI-1640 with 10% FBS, 1% Penicillin-Streptomycin, and 0.05 mM 2-Mercaptoethanol.
    2. Maintain THP-1 cells in a humidified incubator at 37 °C with a 5% CO₂ atmosphere.
    3. Monitor cell density and expand cultures as necessary to maintain viability for downstream assays.

2. **Monocyte-to-Macrophage Differentiation**
    1. Seed THP-1 cells into the desired culture vessel at the required density.
    2. Add 50 ng/mL phorbol-12-myristate 13-acetate (PMA) to the complete growth medium.
    3. Incubate the cells in the PMA-supplemented medium for 3 days to induce differentiation.

3. **Cell Recovery and Verification**
    1. Remove the PMA-containing medium after the 3-day incubation period.
    2. Wash the cells and refeed with fresh complete growth medium (without PMA).
    3. Incubate the cells for an additional 2 days to allow for recovery and maturation.
    4. Verify successful differentiation by observing cell adhesion and spreading morphology using an optical microscope.
