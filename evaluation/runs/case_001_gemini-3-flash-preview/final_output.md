**Source:** Three-dimensional Alginate-bead Culture of Human Pituitary Adenoma Cells

**DOI:** [10.3791/53637](https://doi.org/10.3791/53637)

**Relevance score:** 100.0/100

### Protocol Steps

1. **Preparation of Pituitary Adenoma Cells**
    1. Place human pituitary adenoma tissue in a sterile tube containing cold transport medium (DMEM/F12 supplemented with 10% FBS and antibiotics).
    2. Wash the tissue three times with PBS to remove blood and debris.
    3. Mince the tissue into approximately 1 mm³ pieces using sterile scalpels.
    4. Incubate the minced tissue in a dissociation solution (0.25% trypsin-EDTA or collagenase) at 37 °C for 30–60 minutes with gentle agitation.
    5. Neutralize the enzyme by adding culture medium containing FBS.
    6. Filter the cell suspension through a 70 µm cell strainer to remove undissociated tissue fragments.
    7. Centrifuge the suspension at 300 x g for 5 minutes.
    8. Resuspend the cell pellet in culture medium.
    9. Determine cell viability and concentration using a hemocytometer and trypan blue exclusion.

2. **Alginate Bead Encapsulation**
    1. Prepare a 1.2% (w/v) sodium alginate solution in 0.9% NaCl.
    2. Sterilize the alginate solution using a 0.22 µm filter.
    3. Resuspend the isolated pituitary adenoma cells in the sterile alginate solution to reach a final density of 1–2 x 10⁶ cells/mL.
    4. Draw the cell-alginate mixture into a sterile syringe equipped with a 22G needle.
    5. Drop the mixture slowly into a sterile 102 mM CaCl₂ solution to form beads.
    6. Allow the beads to polymerize in the CaCl₂ solution for 10 minutes at room temperature.
    7. Wash the beads three times with sterile 0.9% NaCl or PBS to remove excess calcium.
    8. Transfer the beads into multi-well plates containing pituitary cell culture medium (DMEM/F12, 10% FBS, and required growth factors).
    9. Incubate the encapsulated cells at 37 °C in a humidified atmosphere with 5% CO₂.

## Rank 2 — Protocol
**Source:** Culture Technique of Human Pituitary Adenoma Cells

**DOI:** [10.1385/0-89603-335-x:447](https://doi.org/10.1385/0-89603-335-x:447)

**Relevance score:** 50.0/100

### Protocol Steps

1. **Tissue Dissociation and Digestion**
    1. Transfer all adenoma tissue to a 60-mm Petri dish using forceps or a pipette.
    2. Mince and crush the tissue thoroughly using scalpels.
    3. Add 0.25% trypsin solution to the dish to suspend the crushed tissues and pipette the mixture several times.
    4. Incubate the suspension for 20 minutes at 37°C, pipetting several times during the incubation period.
    5. Transfer the suspension to a 50-mL centrifuge tube and pipette rapidly approximately 10 times to disperse aggregates.

2. **Cell Filtration and Isolation**
    1. Filter the cell suspension through a cell strainer (Falcon 2350) into a new 50-mL centrifuge tube.
    2. Press any remaining tissue pieces against the strainer mesh using a cotton swab or pipette tip.
    3. Rinse the pressed pieces, strainer, and swab with complete medium to collect all cells.
    4. Centrifuge the filtered suspension at 1500 rpm for 10 minutes to pellet the cells.
    5. Resuspend the pellet in 5 mL of serum-free medium and transfer to a 15-mL centrifuge tube.
    6. Centrifuge at 1000 rpm for 5 minutes; repeat this wash step once.

3. **Erythrocyte Lysis and Final Washing**
    1. Add 2–3 mL of Tris buffer containing $NH_4Cl$ to the cell pellet.
    2. Resuspend the pellet and incubate at room temperature for 2–5 minutes until the solution turns a light red wine color.
    3. Centrifuge the suspension at 1000 rpm for 5 minutes.
    4. Resuspend the pellet in 5 mL of serum-free medium and centrifuge at 1000 rpm for 5 minutes.
    5. Repeat the serum-free medium wash once more to ensure total removal of the lysis buffer.

4. **Seeding and Membrane Culture**
    1. Resuspend the final cell pellet in complete medium to achieve a density of $1–5 \times 10^5$ cells/mL (approximately 5 mL of medium).
    2. Prepare a six-well cell-culture plate by adding 0.5 mL of complete medium to each well.
    3. Place a coated membrane insert into each well, ensuring the outside of the microporous membrane is moistened.
    4. Seed 1 mL of the cell suspension ($1–5 \times 10^5$ cells) into the inside of each membrane insert.
    5. Incubate the plates at 37°C in an atmosphere of 5% $CO_2$ and 95% air.

5. **Maintenance and Media Exchange**
    1. Change the medium in the wells every 3–4 days.
    2. Perform exchange by either transferring the membrane insert to a new well containing 1.5 mL of fresh complete medium or by aspirating the old medium from the well and replacing it with 1.5 mL of fresh medium.
    3. Ensure no medium is added directly to the inside of the membrane insert during exchange.

### Primary Pituitary Adenoma Cell Culture and Isolation Protocol

1.  **Tissue Collection and Preparation**
    1.  Collect pituitary adenoma tumor samples within 30 minutes of surgical resection.
    2.  Wash tumor tissues three times in 1× PBS [[10.1038/sj.bjc.6605142](https://doi.org/10.1038/sj.bjc.6605142)].
    3.  Dissect tissues into 1 mm³ pieces in a dissection medium containing catalase, deferoxamine, N-acetyl cysteine, and superoxide dismutase [[10.1038/sj.bjc.6605142](https://doi.org/10.1038/sj.bjc.6605142)].

2.  **Enzymatic Digestion and Dissociation**
    1.  Digest tissue pieces in papain for 15 minutes at 37 °C.
    2.  Alternatively, digest in trypsin for 10 minutes at 37 °C [[10.1038/sj.bjc.6605142](https://doi.org/10.1038/sj.bjc.6605142)].
    3.  Triturate the samples by passing them through a tissue sieve.
    4.  Filter the resulting suspension through a 70 μm cell strainer to obtain single cells.

3.  **Fluorescence-Activated Cell Sorting (FACS)**
    1.  Wash the single-cell suspension with PBS.
    2.  Resuspend the pellet in FACS buffer.
    3.  Incubate on ice for 30 minutes with anti-CD133 antibody conjugated to APC and anti-S100 antibody conjugated to PE.
    4.  Wash the cells three times with FACS buffer to remove unconjugated antibodies.
    5.  Resuspend the final cell pellet in 200 μl of FACS buffer and perform cell sorting.

4.  **Primary Culture and Expansion**
    1.  Wash sorted cells with DMEM medium.
    2.  Seed cells at a density of 1 × 10⁵ cells/ml in six-well plates [[10.1038/sj.bjc.6605142](https://doi.org/10.1038/sj.bjc.6605142)].
    3.  Culture in stem cell medium: DMEM/F12 supplemented with 10% B27, penicillin/streptomycin (200 U/ml), EGF (40 ng/ml), and bFGF (40 ng/ml).
    4.  Maintain cultures at 37 °C in a humidified atmosphere with 5% CO₂.

5.  **Subsphere Formation Assay (Clonal Analysis)**
    1.  Mechanically dissociate neurospheres into single cells and dilute in culture medium [[10.1038/sj.bjc.6605142](https://doi.org/10.1038/sj.bjc.6605142)].
    2.  Seed cells into 96-well plates at a concentration of one cell per well [[10.1038/sj.bjc.6605142](https://doi.org/10.1038/sj.bjc.6605142)].
    3.  Verify single-cell presence microscopically 2 hours after seeding [[10.1038/sj.bjc.6605142](https://doi.org/10.1038/sj.bjc.6605142)].
    4.  Culture for 2 weeks, refreshing medium every 3 days, and count wells containing subspheres [[10.1038/sj.bjc.6605142](https://doi.org/10.1038/sj.bjc.6605142)].

6.  **Differentiation and Adherent Culture**
    1.  Switch single-cell suspensions to differentiation medium: DMEM/F12 with 2 mM glutamine, 15% horse serum, and 2.5% fetal bovine serum [[10.1038/sj.bjc.6605142](https://doi.org/10.1038/sj.bjc.6605142)].
    2.  Allow cells to attach and grow for 7–10 days [[10.1038/sj.bjc.6605142](https://doi.org/10.1038/sj.bjc.6605142)].
    3.  Alternatively, culture sorted cells in DMEM supplemented with 10% FBS and 200 U/ml penicillin/streptomycin.

7.  **Transwell Co-culture Setup**
    1.  Seed stem cells in the bottom of a 24-well plate in stem cell medium (DMEM/F12, 10% B27, 200 U/ml Pen/Strep, 40 ng/ml EGF, and 40 ng/ml bFGF).
    2.  Coat Transwell inserts with Matrigel.
    3.  Seed HUVEC cells onto the Matrigel-coated inserts in M200 medium supplemented with 10% FBS, 1% Pen/Strep, and LSGS growth factors.
    4.  Incubate the co-culture system at 37 °C with 5% CO₂ for 24 hours.
