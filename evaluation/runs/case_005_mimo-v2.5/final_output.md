2. **Differentiate THP-1 cells into M1 macrophages**:
   - Treat THP-1 cells with 100 ng/mL PMA (Sigma) for 48 hours.
   - Following PMA treatment, stimulate with medium containing 100 ng/mL LPS (Sigma) and 50 ng/mL IFN-γ (R&D Systems) for another 48 hours.
3. **Set up co-culture with UCMSCs**:
   - Seed UCMSCs in the upper chamber of Transwell inserts (0.4 μm pore size, Corning, USA) at a density of 1 × 10^5 cells/well.
   - Place the induced M1 macrophages in the lower chamber.
   - Co-culture for 24 hours.
4. **Collect cells for analysis**:
   - Harvest cells from the lower chamber for gene expression and functional analyses.

# Protocol: Establishment of THP-1 Macrophage Cultures for Immunomodulation Studies

## A. 2D Macrophage Differentiation and Polarization
1.  **Prepare complete culture medium.**
    1.1.  Begin with RPMI-1640 medium.
    1.2.  Supplement with 10% heat-inactivated fetal bovine serum (FBS).
    1.3.  Add the following supplements: 1% penicillin-streptomycin, 10 mM HEPES, 1 mM sodium pyruvate, 2.5 g/L D-glucose, and 50 pM β-mercaptoethanol [{10.1186/s12885-015-1546-9}](https://doi.org/10.1186/s12885-015-1546-9).

2.  **Seed THP-1 cells and induce M0 differentiation.**
    2.1.  Seed THP-1 cells at a density of 1 × 10⁶ cells/mL in untreated 96-well plates.
    2.2.  Add phorbol 12-myristate 13-acetate (PMA) to the medium at a final concentration of 150 nM.
    2.3.  Incubate for 24 hours at 37°C, 5% CO₂ to generate M0 macrophages. Cells designated as M0 are maintained in medium containing PMA.

3.  **Polarize M0 macrophages.**
    3.1.  **For M1 polarization:** Add interferon-gamma (IFN-γ) at 20 ng/mL and lipopolysaccharide (LPS) at 10 pg/mL directly to the existing medium containing PMA.
    3.2.  **For M2 polarization:** Add interleukin-4 (IL-4) at 20 ng/mL and interleukin-13 (IL-13) at 20 ng/mL directly to the existing medium containing PMA. *Note: For optimal M2 marker expression, polarize for 48-72 hours [{10.1186/s12885-015-1546-9}](https://doi.org/10.1186/s12885-015-1546-9).*

4.  **Maintain cultures.**
    4.1.  Replace the medium every two days with fresh complete culture medium containing the same concentrations of PMA and the respective polarization factors (for M1 or M2).

## B. 3D Macrophage Differentiation and Polarization
5.  **Prepare extracellular matrix gel.**
    5.1.  Thaw Matrigel or type I collagen on ice.
    5.2.  Mix THP-1 cells (1 × 10⁶ cells/mL) with the liquid matrix on ice.

6.  **Seed cells in 3D.**
    6.1.  Seed the cell-matrix mixture into 96-well plates.
    6.2.  Allow the gel to solidify at 37°C for 10–20 minutes.

7.  **Induce differentiation and polarization in 3D.**
    7.1.  After gelation, add complete culture medium containing 150 nM PMA on top of the gel.
    7.2.  Incubate for 24 hours to generate M0 macrophages.
    7.3.  For M1 or M2 differentiation, add the corresponding polarization factors (as specified in steps 3.1 and 3.2) to the medium.

## C. Monitoring
8.  **Monitor and image cultures.**
    8.1.  Monitor both 2D and 3D cultures for a total of 7 days.
    8.2.  Perform imaging using a fluorescence microscope.

### Protocol Steps

### 1. Maintain and Expand THP-1 Cell Cultures
    1.1. Seed THP-1 cells (ATCC® TIB-202™) in suspension at a density of 200,000 cells/mL in complete growth medium.
        - **Complete Growth Medium:** RPMI (Cytiva, SH30255) supplemented with 10% fetal bovine serum (FBS; Cytiva, SH30396), 1 mM Sodium Pyruvate (Gibco, 11360-070), and 1% antibiotic-antimycotic (Gibco, 15240062).
    1.2. Maintain cultures in a humidified incubator at 37°C with 5% CO₂.
    1.3. Replace the culture medium every 2–3 days.
    1.4. Passage cells before the culture density reaches 1,000,000 cells/mL.

### 2. Differentiate THP-1 Cells into Macrophages (M0)
    2.1. Plate THP-1 monocytes in a multi-well plate (use 12-well for stimulation experiments; use 24-well for EV uptake experiments).
        - **Seeding Densities:** Seed 400,000 cells per well for a 12-well plate; seed 200,000 cells per well for a 24-well plate.
        - Use complete growth medium (as defined in Step 1.1).
    2.2. Differentiate cells by treating with 100 ng/mL Phorbol 12-myristate 13-acetate (PMA; Cayman, 10008014) for 48 hours.
    2.3. After 48 hours, aspirate the medium to remove non-adherent cells. Gently wash the adherent M0 macrophage layer once with warmed PBS or basal medium.

### 3. Stimulate Macrophages for Immunomodulation Studies (LPS Model)
    3.1. Prepare stimulation medium: RPMI without serum, supplemented with only 1% antibiotic-antimycotic.
    3.2. Treat the adherent M0 macrophages with 500 ng/mL Lipopolysaccharide (LPS; Sigma-Aldrich, L6529) in serum-free stimulation medium for 2 hours to induce an M1 phenotype.
    3.3. After 2-hour LPS priming, add the extracellular vesicle (EV) suspension to the wells.
        - **EV Dosages:** Use a dose of 1,000 particles per cell (e.g., for 400,000 cells/well, add 400,000,000 particles). Alternative dosages for dose-response assays are 500 or 2,000 particles per cell.
    3.4. Incubate the cells for 24 hours under standard culture conditions (37°C, 5% CO₂).
    3.5. At the 24-hour endpoint, collect the cell culture supernatant and the cell pellet (for RNA isolation) as required.

### 4. Assess EV Uptake by Macrophages (Parallel/Control Assay)
    4.1. Follow Step 2 to differentiate THP-1 cells into M0 macrophages in a 24-well plate (200,000 cells/well).
    4.2. Polarize cells to M1 by treating with 500 ng/mL LPS in serum-free medium for 2 hours (as per Step 3.2).
    4.3. Treat the M1 macrophages with PKH26-labeled EVs at a dose of 5,000 particles per cell for 24 hours.
    4.4. After 24 hours, visualize EV uptake using fluorescence microscopy or flow cytometry.

### 5. Experimental Controls
    5.1. Include the following control conditions, processed in parallel with the experimental groups:
        - Negative Control: Differentiated M0 macrophages with no further treatment.
        - LPS-only Control: Differentiated macrophages treated with LPS but no EVs.
        - EV-only Control: Differentiated macrophages treated with EVs but no LPS priming.
